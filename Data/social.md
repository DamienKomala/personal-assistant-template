## Sharing rule — links, not attachments

🔴 **Every outgoing job email carries the resume and impact profile as NAMED HYPERLINKS, not attachments.**

- **Resume** — {{RESUME_DOC_URL}}
- **Impact profile** — {{IMPACT_DOC_URL}}

Both should be link-viewable and both offer PDF and Word download from the File menu. **Present them as named links ("Resume", "Impact profile") alongside the portfolio, and offer to send attachments if the recruiter's system prefers them.**

⚠️ **Why links and not files:** no tool available to these skills can attach a local file. The Gmail connector *can* take base64 inline, **but that costs roughly 63K tokens for this pair on every single email** — which is the whole reason links won. **If a recruiter genuinely insists on real attachments, {{FIRST_NAME}} attaches the local PDFs by hand** from `{{ASSETS_DIR}}/Resumes/`.

🔴 **Never write "attached" unless a file is genuinely attached.**
