## 🔴 The rules of this file

- ✅ **A CHECKBOX MEANS {{FIRST_NAME}} CAN FINISH IT.** Findings, confirmations, patterns and "do NOT" constraints are **plain bullets.** **A checkbox on a fact is a box that never gets ticked**, and a list full of those stops being read.
- ✅ **PRUNE.** Anything sitting untouched for two weeks wasn't real. **A to-do list nobody prunes is a guilt generator.** Sweeps prune as they go.
- 🔴 **IF CLICKUP IS CONFIGURED, EVERY SYNCED CHECKBOX ENDS WITH AN INVISIBLE `<!--cu:xxxxxxxx-->` ANCHOR, AND THAT ANCHOR IS THE ITEM'S IDENTITY.** **When rewriting a carried-forward item, carry its anchor forward with the text.** Drop it and the item re-syncs as a new task while the old one is reported as an orphan. ✅ **New checkboxes need no anchor — the next `--push` stamps them.** **Never invent one, never copy one from another item, never put one on a plain bullet.**
- **Newest sweep block goes at the top of the sweep sections, carrying the `→ **NEWEST**` marker.** Demote the previous one.
